Services

Everything between a good idea and a governed AI workforce.

We cover the full lifecycle of an agent programme, so your operations team has one accountable partner from first workflow to ongoing oversight.

01

Agentic Workflow Design

We map the work your teams repeat every day, then design agents, handoffs and human checkpoints around it.

  • Process discovery and prioritisation by impact and risk
  • Agent roles, tools and escalation rules
  • Human approval steps where they matter

02

Legacy System Integration

Agents are only useful if they can reach your systems. We connect them to the platforms you already run.

  • Connectors for ERP, CRM and records systems
  • Secure access without replacing what works
  • Data mapping, validation and exception handling

03

Ongoing Optimization & Governance

Once agents are live, we keep them accurate, auditable and improving as your work changes.

  • Accuracy, cost and exception monitoring
  • Audit trails, access controls and policy reviews
  • Regular tuning and quarterly performance reporting

Process

Audit, build, deploy, monitor.

A clear path from the first conversation to an AI workforce that your team can trust and your leadership can inspect.

  1. Audit

    We shadow the work, map every handoff and rank workflows by impact and risk.

    Output: a prioritised automation roadmap.

  2. Build

    We build agents and test them against your real cases, with approvals and escalation designed in.

    Output: tested agents in a staging environment.

  3. Deploy

    We connect agents to your systems, train your people and roll out in stages.

    Output: agents working live alongside your team.

  4. Monitor

    We track accuracy, cost and exceptions, report on results and tune as your work changes.

    Output: a governed workforce that keeps improving.
