About

Practical AI from Canadian telecom veterans

Hailant was started by Canadian telecom veterans who watched the hype around AI grow while small businesses were left with little professional guidance.

Why we started Hailant

The hype got ahead of the help.

AI is promoted everywhere, but a small business that wants to use it has few places to turn for professional guidance, and few tools built with its situation in mind. Hailant exists to change that.

We build for Canadian businesses. That shapes how we handle data, who can see what, and how we judge whether the work is paying off.

Where we focus

What small businesses are missing.

Four gaps come up again and again. Each one shapes how we design, build and run AI for the businesses we work with.

Data sovereignty and compliance

Many AI tools leave Canadian small businesses unsure where their data goes and whether they are meeting Canadian legal requirements. We treat data sovereignty and compliance as the starting point of every project, not something added at the end.

Information kept in the right place

Small teams still need to separate business information properly, and most tools do not support permissions-based knowledge segregation. We organize your information into separate, appropriately permissioned stores, so people and agents reach only what they should.

Memory that holds up

Knowledge graphs in most AI tools are rudimentary, and agent memory is substandard. We build on stronger knowledge graphs and memory, so agents can keep track of how your business actually works.

Steps that lead to revenue

A lot of AI advice stops at what is possible. We focus on actionable steps directed at revenue, so each piece of work points toward growing your business.
