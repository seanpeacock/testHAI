About

Practical AI help for busy small businesses

Everyone is talking about AI. Few people are helping small businesses use it well. Hailant was started by Canadian telecom veterans to change that.

Why we started Hailant

The hype got ahead of the help.

You hear about AI every day. Few people can tell you where to start, and fewer build tools with a small team in mind.

Hailant agents take on repetitive steps, like intake, reminders, and follow-ups. Your team reviews anything that matters.

Agents can get things wrong. That's why unusual cases come to a person first.

Where we focus

Four things we look after first.

Careful owners ask about these first, so we start here.

Where your data goes

Many AI tools don't say where your data is kept. They're also vague about Canadian legal requirements. We start every project with both questions.

Who can see what

Small teams still need to keep some information separate. We sort your business information into separate areas. Each agent sees only what its job needs, and you decide what that is.

Memory that holds up

Most AI tools don't keep track of how your business works. We build agents with better memory. That way, everyone on your team answers from the same, current picture.

Steps that lead to revenue

Plenty of AI advice stops at what's possible. We focus on practical next steps that lead to revenue, so you can see what each one is for.
