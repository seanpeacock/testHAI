Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 16, 2026 · Last updated: April 16, 2026

1. Who We Are

Hailant Technologies Inc. ("Hailant," "we," "us," or "our") is an AI solutions provider incorporated in Alberta, Canada. We deliver AI-powered business tools for Canadian SMBs, built on Anthropic's Claude platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information in connection with our services and website (hailant.ca).

We are committed to complying with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation.

2. Anthropic's Privacy Policy

Hailant's services are powered by Anthropic's Claude AI. When you use our AI-powered features, your data may be processed by Anthropic's systems. Anthropic maintains its own privacy practices governing how it handles data submitted through Claude.

Anthropic Privacy Policy

To understand how Anthropic collects and processes data through Claude, please review Anthropic's full privacy policy:

privacy.claude.com/en/ ↗

By using Hailant's AI-powered services, you also agree to Anthropic's privacy practices as described at the link above.

3. Information We Collect

We collect personal information in the following ways:

Information you provide directly:

  • Name, business email, company name (via contact or sign-up forms)
  • Billing and payment information (processed securely through our payment provider)
  • Messages and content you submit through our platform or AI interface
  • Support requests and communications

Information collected automatically:

  • Website usage data (pages visited, time spent, referring URLs)
  • Device and browser information (type, OS, IP address)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 8)

Information from third parties:

  • Data from integrated business systems you authorize us to connect to (CRM, email, etc.)

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our AI-powered services
  • Process transactions and send related administrative communications
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • Send service updates, security alerts, and account notifications
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our platform
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Anthropic PBC — for AI processing through Claude (subject to Anthropic's privacy policy)
  • Service providers — payment processors, cloud hosting, analytics tools — who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations
  • Legal authorities — when required by applicable law, court order, or to protect our legal rights
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to affected users

6. Canadian Data Residency

Hailant is a Canadian company and we prioritize keeping your data within Canada where technically and contractually feasible. However, some of our service providers (including Anthropic) may process data outside of Canada, including in the United States. By using our services, you consent to this cross-border transfer where necessary to provide the service.

We take reasonable contractual and technical measures to ensure data processed outside Canada receives comparable privacy protections.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Specifically:

  • Account data: retained for the duration of your account plus 2 years
  • Billing records: retained for 7 years as required by Canadian tax law
  • AI conversation logs: retained for up to 90 days unless you request earlier deletion
  • Website analytics: retained in aggregated form indefinitely; identifiable data for 26 months

You may request deletion of your personal information at any time (see Section 10).

8. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you signed in and remember your preferences
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Improve site performance and user experience

You can control cookie settings through your browser. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including encryption in transit (TLS), access controls, and regular security reviews. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable Canadian law.

10. Your Rights

Under PIPEDA and applicable provincial law, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — request that inaccurate information be corrected
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Withdrawal of consent — withdraw consent to certain uses of your data (which may limit your ability to use our services)
  • Complaint — lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC) at priv.gc.ca

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@hailant.ca.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or by posting a prominent notice on our website at least 14 days before the changes take effect. Continued use of our services after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

13. Links to Third-Party Sites

Our website and services may contain links to third-party websites, including Anthropic's privacy portal. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies independently.
